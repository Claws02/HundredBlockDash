Need help? Email [[SUPPORT EMAIL]]. We usually reply within [[RESPONSE TIME, e.g. 2 business days]]. Tell us your device, the app version (Settings, bottom of the screen) and what happened.

Purchases

Something I bought isn't unlocked. Open the Shop or Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

I want a refund. Refunds are handled by the store: on iPhone/iPad use reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, or see Google's refund help.

My Tickets disappeared. Tickets live only on your device, so clearing the app's data or reinstalling resets them. Anything bought with money comes back with Restore Purchases.

Ads and privacy

Ads only play when you tap "watch an ad". To change your ad privacy choice, open Settings → Privacy choices. Read the Privacy Policy.

Online play

Friends can't join my room. All phones need an internet connection. Some networks (often school, work or hotel Wi-Fi) block phone-to-phone connections; try mobile data on one phone. Friends join free and play everything the host has unlocked.

Report a problem player

Rooms are private and there is no chat. If someone misuses a player name, leave the room and only share codes with people you know. Tell us about anything serious at the email above.
