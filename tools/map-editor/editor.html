How to use the editor

AddTap a model in the library. It lands in the middle of the view, selected. MoveDrag a building with one finger. TurnDrag the gold handle. It marks the building's front; fronts should face the road. Look aroundDrag empty ground to pan (orbit in 3D). Pinch to zoom. Change itVariant, HQ, size and exact position are in the panel at the bottom. RedThe building overlaps another, or stands on a space or the road. SaveSave for Claude stores the layout where Claude can read it. Your work is also kept on this device between saves. Keys⌫ delete · R turn 15° · D duplicate · ⌘Z undo · ⇧⌘Z redo
