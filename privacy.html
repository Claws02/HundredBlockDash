What stays on your device

The game keeps these in your device's local storage. We never receive them:

  • your settings (sound, music, text size, reduced motion, battery saver) and privacy choices;
  • your Ticket balance, what you have unlocked and which cosmetics you have equipped;
  • a record of the purchases this device has made, so unlocks work offline;
  • the names you type for players, and your last online name;
  • a match in progress, so you can resume it (deleted when the match ends, when you quit, or after 7 days);
  • simple counters, such as your win record and how many bonus ads you have watched today;
  • which tips and introductions you have already seen;
  • a short log of the last 20 errors the game hit, for troubleshooting. It never leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes all of it. Purchases you paid for can be brought back with Restore Purchases; Tickets and Ticket-bought unlocks cannot, because we keep no copy of them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see your name, email address or payment details. The store gives the app a purchase receipt, which the app checks on your device to unlock what you bought.

Optional ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows an ad only when you choose to watch one for bonus Tickets. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play.

When you choose to watch one, Google AdMob (Google LLC) serves it. To serve the ad, measure it and prevent fraud, Google may collect: your device's advertising identifier (on Android) or other device identifiers, your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information (model, OS, language, app version), and how you interact with the ad. Google's use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • No cross-app tracking on iPhone/iPad. We do not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so the iOS advertising identifier is never shared.
  • Consent in the UK, EEA and Switzerland. Before any ad is requested, you are asked whether ads may be personalised. You can say no and still watch ads (they will be non-personalised) or not watch ads at all.
  • US state privacy rights. Where the law treats sharing for targeted advertising as a "sale" or "share", you can opt out with Settings → Privacy choices.
  • You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices, and reset your Android advertising ID in your device settings.
  • If the store tells the app that you are under 18 in a place where the law requires it, the ad and purchase buttons are hidden.

Online play

When you host or join an online room, the game uses public matchmaking services (Nostr relays, or BitTorrent trackers as a fallback) only to introduce the phones in the room to each other. Those services see an encrypted room identifier and your IP address, which is how the internet works. They do not see your name or your game.

After that, gameplay travels directly between the phones over an encrypted WebRTC connection. The other players in your room see the name you type, the cosmetics you have equipped, which minigames the host has unlocked, and your moves. Nothing is stored after the match.

If a direct connection is impossible, the connection may pass through a relay server (TURN). The relay forwards encrypted traffic and cannot read it.

Age signals

Where the law requires it (for example Texas, Utah and Louisiana), the app asks Apple or Google for your age range and whether a parent has approved. It uses the answer only to decide whether to show purchases and ads, keeps it in memory only, and never sends it to us or anyone else.

Crash reports

This version does not send crash reports. If a future version does, it will be limited to technical details (error message, device model, OS and app version), with player names removed, and this policy will be updated first.

Children

Hundred Block Dash is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There is no chat, no account and no way to contact other players except through the room code you share yourself. If you believe a child has watched an ad that collected data, contact us and we will help you reset it through Google.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data on our side, most requests are answered by clearing the app's data. For ad data, use the Privacy choices button or Google's tools; we will help with any request at the address above.

Legal bases (UK/EEA): your settings and progress are kept to provide the game you asked for (contract); ads are personalised only with your consent, and non-personalised ads rely on our legitimate interest in funding a free game.
